How Match Levels Work Show / hide

Match levels are a guide, not a final verdict. The raw score is kept internally for sorting and test mode, while normal mode uses human-friendly bands so the dashboard does not pretend to be more precise than it really is.

Strong match: strongest fit signalsGood match: clear fit with fewer caveatsPossible fit: plausible fit worth reviewingStretch: lower-confidence or borderline fitTitle match: direct titles are favored over secondary titlesDescription review: stronger description fit lifts the match levelCompetitive signals: specialist bias can lift or lower the match levelFreshness: newer roles are favoredDecision weights: fit, pay, location, work mode, contract, government, and freshness can be dialed up or downWatchouts: essential gaps hit harder than desirable-only gapsRisks: essential gaps hit harder than desirable-only gaps
