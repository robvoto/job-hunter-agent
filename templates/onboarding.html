How this works

This creates the profile Job Hunter uses to find and score relevant jobs for you.

  1. You upload one detailed CV.
  2. We extract likely titles, strengths, and a starter search direction.
  3. You review the draft, set search basics, and confirm it before matching starts.
Required. Use the most detailed CV you have, not the prettiest final layout.
Privacy tip: only upload the CV content needed for matching. Remove date of birth, licence numbers, passport details, street address, and other sensitive personal identifiers.
What makes a good CV for setup?

Best results come from a detailed CV with role titles, dates, employers, and bullet points.

Include

  • Recent and older roles with dates
  • Clear job titles
  • Employer names
  • Bullet points describing what you did
  • Skills, tools, domains, or platforms you actually used

Avoid

  • One-page marketing CVs with very little detail
  • Image-heavy layouts where the text is hard to extract
  • CVs with missing dates or vague role names
  • Multiple different target directions crammed into a short summary

Plain, detailed content beats pretty formatting.
