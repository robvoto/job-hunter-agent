How this works
  1. You upload one detailed CV.
  2. We extract likely job titles, capabilities, and a starter search direction.
  3. You review the draft, set search basics, and confirm it before matching starts.

Drag & drop your CV here, or click to browse

Supported formats: .docx, .pdf, .md, .txt

Privacy & sensitive information

We take privacy seriously.

Upload only the CV content needed for job matching.

Avoid including sensitive personal identifiers such as:

  • passport numbers
  • licence numbers
  • street address
  • date of birth
  • national identity numbers

Basic contact details like your name, email address, and phone number are generally expected in a CV.

When processing your CV, sensitive personal identifiers are not intentionally stored as part of profile matching data.

Your CV itself is not stored in our system. We only extract and retain relevant job-related information to build your profile and match you with suitable roles.

What makes a good CV for setup?

Include

  • Recent and older roles with dates
  • Clear job titles
  • Employer names
  • Bullet points describing what you did
  • Skills, tools, domains, or platforms you actually used

Avoid

  • One-page marketing CVs with very little detail
  • Image-heavy layouts where the text is hard to extract
  • CVs with missing dates or vague role names
  • Multiple different target directions crammed into a short summary

Plain, detailed content beats pretty formatting.
