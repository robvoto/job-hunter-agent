What makes a good CV for setup?

Best results come from a detailed CV with role titles, dates, employers, and bullet points.

Include

  • Recent and older roles with dates
  • Clear job titles
  • Employer names
  • Bullet points describing what you did
  • Skills, tools, domains, or platforms you actually used

Avoid

  • One-page marketing CVs with very little detail
  • Image-heavy layouts where the text is hard to extract
  • CVs with missing dates or vague role names
  • Multiple different target directions crammed into a short summary

Primary job titles

You can confidently do these roles based on your past experience. These are treated as high-confidence matches.

Secondary job titles

Only include roles where you can prove the fit through specific experience. These require validation before being accepted.

Capability matrix

These are the capabilities Job Hunter picked up from your CV. The capability name is for you. Aliases are alternate terms it can recognise in job ads. Job Hunter should usually suggest these for you.
