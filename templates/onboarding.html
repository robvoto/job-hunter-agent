What makes a good CV for setup?

Best results come from a detailed CV with role titles, dates, employers, and bullet points.

Include

  • Recent and older roles with dates
  • Clear job titles
  • Employer names
  • Bullet points describing what you did
  • Skills, tools, domains, or platforms you actually used

Avoid

  • One-page marketing CVs with very little detail
  • Image-heavy layouts where the text is hard to extract
  • CVs with missing dates or vague role names
  • Multiple different target directions crammed into a short summary
