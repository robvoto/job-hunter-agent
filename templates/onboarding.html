How this works

Privacy & sensitive information

We take privacy seriously.

Upload only the CV content needed for job matching.

Avoid including sensitive personal identifiers such as:

  • passport numbers
  • licence numbers
  • street address
  • date of birth
  • national identity numbers

Your uploaded CV is not stored. We only extract temporary job-related information needed to match you with suitable roles.

What makes a good CV for setup?

Include

  • Recent and older roles with dates
  • Clear job titles
  • Employer names
  • Bullet points describing what you did
  • Skills, tools, domains, or platforms you actually used

Avoid

  • One-page marketing CVs with very little detail
  • Image-heavy layouts where the text is hard to extract
  • CVs with missing dates or vague role names
  • Multiple different target directions crammed into a short summary

Plain, detailed content beats pretty formatting.
