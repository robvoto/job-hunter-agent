How capability suggestions work

Job Hunter finds repeated capability signals in jobs you keep. Confirm adds a capability to Matching → Capability Matrix, where you can edit it or add capabilities yourself. If you do not have it, choose “I don't have this”. If it is not useful, choose “Ignore suggestion”; ignored suggestions are saved and will not be suggested again.

Suggestions appear after Job Hunter has kept jobs to learn from.
