Signal categories explained
Capability concept
A skill or technology to track in job ads and your profile (e.g. "stakeholder engagement", "Power BI").
Government context
A government-specific term — agency, clearance level, or APS grade (e.g. "baseline clearance", "EL2").
Government context pattern
A structural clearance or agency pattern using [*] as a wildcard (e.g. NV[*] clearance). Use when the exact value varies but the shape is fixed.
Role title token
A word or short phrase that signals a job title (e.g. "analyst", "coordinator"). Used to recognise titles in your CV and in job ads.
Role title pattern
A structural title shape using [*] as a wildcard (e.g. Head of [*]). Use when the role type is fixed but the domain varies.
Title normalization candidate
An abbreviation or alternate spelling to map to a canonical title (e.g. "BA" → "Business Analyst").
Hard blocker pattern
A phrase that disqualifies a job outright (e.g. "must hold NV2 clearance"). Approved blockers filter jobs before scoring.
CV farming pattern
A phrase or pattern that suggests a job ad is collecting CVs rather than filling a real role.
